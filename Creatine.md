# 1. Creatine

**Formula:** C₄H₉N₃O₂  
**Molecular Weight:** 131.13 g/mol  
**CAS:** 57-00-1

Creatine is a naturally occurring compound synthesized from arginine, glycine, and methionine. About 95% is stored in skeletal muscle as phosphocreatine (PCr). It is one of the most researched performance supplements available.

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Effect |
|----------|---------|
| Strength Gain | +8–14% |
| Power Output | +15–20% |
| Cognitive Support | Up to +20% |
| Water Retention | 0.5–2 kg intramuscularly |

## Recommended Dosage

| Protocol | Dose |
|-----------|------|
| Standard | 3–5 g/day |
| Loading | 20 g/day (4×5 g) for 5–7 days |
| High Dose | 10 g/day |
| Cycling | Not required |

## Best Form

**Creatine Monohydrate** remains the gold standard due to effectiveness, research support, and affordability.

### Evidence Scores

| Form | Score |
|------|--------|
| Monohydrate | 10/10 |
| Micronized Monohydrate | 9.5/10 |
| Nitrate | 7/10 |
| HCl | 6/10 |
| Kre-Alkalyn | 5/10 |
| Ethyl Ester | 4/10 |

# 2. Creatinine

**Formula:** C₄H₇N₃O

Creatinine is a waste product formed when creatine and phosphocreatine break down in muscle tissue.

## Normal Blood Levels

| Group | Range |
|---------|---------|
| Male | 0.7–1.3 mg/dL |
| Female | 0.6–1.1 mg/dL |

## Important Notes

- Creatine supplementation may increase serum creatinine slightly.
- This does **not automatically indicate kidney damage**.
- Athletes often have higher creatinine due to increased muscle mass.

# 4. Timing Protocol

## Morning

- Vitamin D3 + K2
- Omega-3
- Creatine (5 g)
- High-protein breakfast

## Pre-Workout (60 min)

- Caffeine
- Citrulline Malate
- Beta-Alanine

## During Workout

- Water & electrolytes
- Optional BCAA for fasted training

## Post-Workout

- Whey Protein (25–40 g)
- Carbohydrates
- Optional Glutamine

## Before Bed

- Casein Protein
- Zinc
- Magnesium
- Ashwagandha

# 7. Safety Notes

## Key Rule

> Sleep → Nutrition → Training → Supplementation

Supplements cannot compensate for:

- Poor diet
- Poor sleep
- Ineffective training

This guide is for educational purposes only. Consult a qualified physician, sports nutritionist, or registered dietitian before starting supplementation.
