# 1. Neurochemistry Map

Understanding the six key performance chemicals:

| Molecule       | Function               | Trigger                            | Effect               |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Adrenaline     | Emergency fuel         | Cold exposure, exercise, challenge | Hyper-focus, urgency |
| Norepinephrine | Attention amplifier    | Novelty, challenge                 | Sustained focus      |
| Dopamine       | Motivation engine      | Progress, goals, rewards           | Drive & learning     |
| Cortisol       | Productive stress      | Deadlines, fasting, exercise       | Performance boost    |
| Acetylcholine  | Focus glue             | Visual focus, caffeine             | Deep concentration   |
| Serotonin      | Stability & confidence | Sunlight, exercise                 | Emotional stability  |

### Optimal Study State

* Adrenaline → High
* Norepinephrine → High
* Dopamine → Moderate-High
* Cortisol → Moderate
* Acetylcholine → High
* Serotonin → Stable

# 2. Adrenaline Triggers

### Physical

Activate the sympathetic nervous system:

* Cold water
* Intense exercise
* Breath holds

### Psychological

Increase perceived stakes:

* Deadlines
* Public accountability
* Consequences of failure

### Cognitive

Work on tasks slightly beyond current skill level:

* Aim for ~4% difficulty beyond comfort zone

### Rhythmic

Work in natural 90-minute ultradian cycles.

# 3. Physical Activation Stack

## 1. Cold Water Face Immersion (30 sec)

Benefits:

* Activates vagus nerve
* Releases norepinephrine
* Sharpens focus

Effect duration:

* 90–120 minutes

---

## 2. Explosive 10-Minute Exercise

Example:

* 50 Jumping Jacks
* 20 Pushups
* 30 sec Sprint
* 10 Burpees

Benefits:

* Adrenaline surge
* BDNF release
* Improved cognition

---

## 3. Physiological Sigh

Method:

1. Deep inhale
2. Short second inhale
3. Long exhale

Benefits:

* Reduces anxiety
* Maintains alertness

---

## 4. Power Posture (2 min)

Benefits:

* Increased confidence
* Lower stress

---

## 5. Strategic Caffeine

Guidelines:

* Delay 90–120 min after waking
* Best usage: 1–3 PM
* Combine with L-Theanine
* Max: 400 mg/day
* Avoid after 2 PM

# 11. Anti-Patterns
